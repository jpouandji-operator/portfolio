Notes
Figures are means, not medians. Provable from the tables themselves: an all-firms value often sits below every size-band value, which a median cannot do.

Column order in every profile is All Firms, Small, Medium, Large, Regional average, Income-group average. The extractor relies on that order.

"n.a." is preserved as blank, never as zero. A missing indicator and a value of nothing are different facts.

Sample excludes firms under five employees, informal firms, fully state-owned firms, and the financial intermediation sector entirely. Nothing here describes fintech licensing.
